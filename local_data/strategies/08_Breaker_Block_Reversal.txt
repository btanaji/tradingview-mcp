NAME: Breaker_Block_Reversal
DESCRIPTION: Reversal entry on the retest of an order block that has fully failed (price closed through its extreme before reversing)
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 4h, 1h (identification), 15m, 5m (entry)
BREAKER_DEFINITION:
- Start with a candidate order block as defined in OB_Continuation.
- The order block is reclassified as a breaker block only if a subsequent candle's body (the close price, not the wick) trades beyond the order block's far extreme, invalidating it, and this close is followed by a structure shift in the opposite direction.
- If price only wicks through the order block's extreme without a body close beyond it, the block remains a valid order block (see OB_Continuation and Mitigation_Block) and must not be treated as a breaker.
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- wick_reject: price retests the broken order block, wicks into it, and closes back out in the new direction — the primary trigger, matching the "retest and reject" nature of a breaker
- close_beyond: entry-timeframe candle closes beyond its own most recent minor swing point while retesting the broken order block — a stronger-confirmation alternative to wick_reject
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop placed beyond the breaker block's far extreme, on the side opposite the new trade direction — the primary stop
- fixed_atr: stop placed 1.5x ATR(14) beyond the breaker block's far extreme — use when the breaker block itself is unusually small
(all modes: clamp to broker/data minimum stop distance if too tight)
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- fixed_2R: full close at a fixed 2R — the base case
- partial_1_5R: partial close at 1.5R, remainder to breakeven and trailed by new swing structure toward the next liquidity pool or opposing PD array — the standard choice when the reversal has strong HTF support
FILTERS:
- Skip entry if the invalidating close (per BREAKER_DEFINITION) cannot be confirmed as a body close beyond the extreme using only a wick — this is the single most common source of breaker/mitigation misclassification and must be checked explicitly, not assumed
- Skip entry if there is no higher-timeframe (4h/1d) support for the new direction
NOTES:
- Mirror case of Mitigation_Block. Both share the same retest mechanic; this file applies when the order block fails, Mitigation_Block applies when it holds.
