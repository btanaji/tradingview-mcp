NAME: BPR_FVG_Overlap
DESCRIPTION: Entry at the overlap of two opposite-direction fair value gaps (a Balanced Price Range)
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 15m, 1h (identification), 5m, 1m (entry)
BPR_DEFINITION:
- Identify a bearish fair value gap (per FVG_Rebalance's definition) and a bullish fair value gap that overlap at the same price range, formed by two opposite fast moves in close succession.
- The overlapping price range between the two gaps is the Balanced Price Range (BPR). Its center point (the midpoint of the overlap) is the primary reference level.
- A BPR is valid only if the overlap between the two gaps is at least 30% of the smaller gap's total range. Overlaps below this threshold are too thin to trade.
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- wick_reject: price returns to the BPR, wicks into it, and closes back outside it in the trade direction — the primary trigger, matching the sharp-reaction nature of a two-gap overlap
- close_beyond: entry-timeframe candle closes beyond its own most recent minor swing point while inside the BPR — a stronger-confirmation alternative
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop placed beyond the BPR's far edge, treated as the relevant structural swing point for this setup — the primary stop
- fixed_atr: stop placed 1.2x ATR(14) beyond the BPR's far edge — use when the BPR itself is unusually narrow
(all modes: clamp to broker/data minimum stop distance if too tight)
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- fixed_2R: full close at a fixed 2R — the base case
- partial_1_5R: partial close at 1.5R, remainder to breakeven and trailed toward the next liquidity pool — the standard choice, given the BPR's tighter stop typically supports a larger trailed target than a single FVG
FILTERS:
- Skip entry if the overlap between the two gaps is below the 30% threshold defined in BPR_DEFINITION
- Skip entry if there is no higher-timeframe (1h) trend support for the trade direction
NOTES:
- Stricter, two-gap version of FVG_Rebalance. Use this file's tighter stop-loss convention given the higher-precision entry zone.
