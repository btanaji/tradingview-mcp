NAME: Killzone_Intraday
DESCRIPTION: Restricts liquidity-sweep-and-reversal entries to defined intraday session windows
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 15m (session range), 5m, 1m (entry)
SESSION_WINDOWS (broker server time must be converted to EST before applying these):
- London window: 02:00-05:00 EST
- New York window (forex pairs and metals): 07:00-10:00 EST
- New York window (indices): 08:30-11:00 EST
- Asian window (only if trading AUD/NZD/JPY pairs): 20:00-00:00 EST
- London Close window (optional, lower priority): 10:00-12:00 EST
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- close_beyond: a liquidity sweep of the prior session's or Asian range's high/low is followed by a candle close beyond the pre-sweep swing point (per Liq_Sweep_Reversal's CONFIRMATION_SEQUENCE), occurring inside one of the SESSION_WINDOWS — the primary trigger for this strategy
- wick_reject: the sweep candle itself closes back inside the range within the same SESSION_WINDOW, without waiting for a separate confirmation candle — a faster alternative to close_beyond
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop placed beyond the sweep candle's wick extreme — the primary stop
- fixed_atr: stop placed 1.5x ATR(14) beyond the sweep candle's wick extreme — use when the sweep wick is unusually small
(all modes: clamp to broker/data minimum stop distance if too tight)
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- fixed_2R: full close at a fixed 2R — the base case for this strategy
- partial_1_5R: partial close at 1.5R, remainder to breakeven and trailed toward the next liquidity pool — the standard choice; see FILTERS for the forced session-end exit that applies on top of whichever mode is selected
- trail_only: no fixed target, trail behind new swing points once 1R is reached; also subject to the forced session-end exit in FILTERS
FILTERS:
- Skip entry entirely if current server time, converted to EST, falls outside all defined SESSION_WINDOWS
- Skip entry if a scheduled high-impact news release falls inside the active SESSION_WINDOW
- Skip entry on the trading day before, or the trading day of, a recognized market holiday
- Force-close any still-open position at the end of the active SESSION_WINDOW regardless of which TAKE_PROFIT mode is selected — do not hold into the dead period between sessions (this replaces the previous "session_close_exit" TAKE_PROFIT label, which is not part of the recognized token set)
NOTES:
- Session window boundaries are commonly cited within +/- 30-60 minutes of these values across different educators, and shift with US/UK daylight saving. Verify against your broker's actual candle timestamps before relying on these exact numbers in live trading.
