NAME: FVG_Inducement
DESCRIPTION: Liquidity sweep + MSS entry, refined by requiring a minor internal swing point within the displacement leg (inducement/IDM) to also be swept before the resulting FVG is treated as a valid entry
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 1h, 4h (sweep/MSS identification), 15m (inducement + FVG entry)
ZONES:
- Identify an external liquidity sweep (per Liq_Sweep_Reversal's LIQUIDITY_POOLS definition) followed by a close beyond the pre-sweep opposite swing (MSS confirmation).
- Within that displacement leg (sweep candle to MSS confirmation candle), identify minor internal swing points (a 3-bar fractal, since displacement legs are often too short for a 5-bar fractal to register a pivot).
- Inducement (IDM): the setup only becomes valid once a subsequent candle sweeps one of these internal swing points.
- The tradeable zone is the Fair Value Gap (3-candle gap) left within the displacement leg, entered only after the IDM sweep confirms.
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- close_beyond: a candle closes beyond the FVG's near edge in the trade direction after the IDM sweep
- wick_reject: the IDM-sweep candle itself closes back through the FVG by at least 50% of its own range
- midpoint_close: a candle closes beyond the FVG's 50% midpoint in the trade direction
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop beyond the IDM sweep candle's wick extreme
- opposing_candle: stop beyond the wick extreme of the entry/confirmation candle itself
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- fixed_2R: full close at 2R
- partial_1_5R: partial at 1.5R, remainder trailed by new swing points
- trail_only: trail behind new swing points once 1R is reached
FILTERS:
- Skip if no internal swing point can be identified within the displacement leg (leg too short/too few candles).
- Skip if the IDM sweep does not occur within 20 candles of the MSS confirmation.
NOTES:
- Source: algostorm.com/ict-smc-entry-models/, "Model 2: FVG + Internal Liquidity (Inducement)". The source page is a critical/skeptical analysis of ICT methodology and explicitly does not define SL/TP rules for this model — the STOP_LOSS/TAKE_PROFIT options above reuse this server's existing generic vocabulary rather than being sourced from the page itself.
- Distinct from Liq_Sweep_Reversal only in the added internal-sweep (IDM) gate before FVG entry; compare backtest results between the two to see whether the extra filter helps or just reduces trade count.
- Runs on the ICT registry engine (zones_fvg_inducement in ict_strategies.py) — see interpreted_rules.zone_source in backtest results to confirm.
