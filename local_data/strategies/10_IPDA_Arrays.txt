NAME: IPDA_Arrays
DESCRIPTION: Trades reactions at the boundaries of the trailing 20/40/60 trading-day high-low ranges
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 1d (range calculation), 4h, 1h (entry)
DATA_RANGES:
- Calculate three ranges on the daily chart: the highest high and lowest low of the trailing 20 trading days, the trailing 40 trading days, and the trailing 60 trading days.
- Split each range at its 50% midpoint to define premium/discount/equilibrium within that specific range.
- Treat the boundary (high or low) of each range as a PD array reference level, to be combined with order block / fair value gap confluence per ENTRY_CONFIRMATION.
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- close_beyond: price reaches a range boundary, sweeps a minor swing point at that level, and a 4h/1h candle closes beyond the pre-sweep swing point in the reversal direction — the primary trigger
- wick_reject: price sweeps the range boundary, then retests the resulting order block, wicking into it and closing back out in the reversal direction — replaces an open-ended retest wait with a defined reject-and-continue trigger
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop placed beyond the wick extreme of the candle that swept the range boundary — the primary stop
- fixed_atr: stop placed 1.5x ATR(14) beyond the range boundary — use when the sweep wick is small relative to the size of the 20/40/60-day range
(all modes: clamp to broker/data minimum stop distance if too tight)
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- partial_1_5R: partial close at 1.5R, remainder to breakeven and trailed toward the next range boundary — the primary choice, mirroring the original design of stepping toward the 20-day, then 40-day, then 60-day boundary in order of distance
- trail_only: no fixed target, trail behind new swing points once 1R is reached — for the highest-conviction 60-day boundary setups, where the eventual target is furthest away
FILTERS:
- Do not treat any percentage subdivision within each range beyond the 50% midpoint (including any Goldbach or prime-number based percentage mapping) as validated. Only the 20/40/60-day high-low boundaries and their 50% midpoints are used by this file. Any additional percentage-level overlay must be separately backtested before being added.
- Skip entry if fewer than 60 trading days of price history are available to calculate all three ranges
NOTES:
- The 20/40/60-day lookback periods are ICT's original IPDA framework. A separate practitioner extension maps these ranges to Goldbach-conjecture-derived percentage splits; that overlay is intentionally excluded from this file pending independent verification.
