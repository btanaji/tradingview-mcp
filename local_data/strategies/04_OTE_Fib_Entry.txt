NAME: OTE_Fib_Entry
DESCRIPTION: Entry inside the 62%-79% Fibonacci retracement zone of a confirmed impulsive swing, with required confluence
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 4h, 1h (swing identification), 15m, 5m (entry)
FIBONACCI_ZONE:
- Identify the most recent impulsive swing on the 4h/1h chart. For a bullish read, the swing low is the anchor at 0% and the most recent swing high is the anchor at 100%. For a bearish read, this is reversed (swing high = 0%, swing low = 100%).
- Plot the retracement using candle body highs/lows as the anchor points, not wicks, to reduce sensitivity to broker-specific spread and wick noise.
- The Optimal Trade Entry zone is the price range between the 62% and 79% retracement levels of that swing. The 70.5% level is the designated midpoint reference within that zone.
- The setup is invalid if price retraces beyond the 100% anchor (the original swing low/high) before reaching the OTE zone. In that case the swing being measured has failed and must be redefined from scratch.
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- close_beyond: a 5m candle closes beyond its own most recent minor swing point while price is inside the 62%-79% zone — the primary trigger for this strategy
- wick_reject: price wicks into the 62%-79% zone and closes back out of it in the trend direction — a faster alternative to close_beyond
- midpoint_close: a 5m candle closes beyond the 70.5% level specifically (the designated midpoint of the 62%-79% zone) in the trend direction — replaces an unconfirmed resting limit order at 70.5% with a confirmed trigger at that same level, since the parser requires a triggering candle
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop placed beyond the 100% anchor of the measured swing — the primary stop, since a retrace past 100% invalidates the swing being measured
- fixed_atr: stop placed 1.5x ATR(14) beyond the 100% anchor — use when the 100% anchor sits unusually close to the 79% level, which would otherwise give too tight a stop
(all modes: clamp to broker/data minimum stop distance if too tight)
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- fixed_2R: full close at a fixed 2R — the conservative choice for this strategy
- partial_1_5R: partial close at 1.5R, remainder to breakeven and trailed toward the 0% anchor and beyond — the standard choice, since OTE's tight stop relative to the swing's full length typically supports a larger trailed target
- trail_only: no fixed target, trail behind new swing points once 1R is reached — for the highest-conviction setups where the 0% anchor is expected to be exceeded
FILTERS:
- Skip entry if there is no order block, fair value gap, or liquidity sweep present inside the 62%-79% zone — a bare Fibonacci touch with no other confluence is not a valid entry
- Skip entry if the measured swing is younger than 3 candles on the 4h/1h chart (insufficient data to confirm it is a genuine impulsive move)
NOTES:
- Uses the same 62%-79% zone definition and 70.5% sweet spot referenced across the rest of this rulebook.
