NAME: Liq_FVG_Combo
DESCRIPTION: Liquidity sweep reversal entered specifically at the midpoint of the fair value gap the reversal displacement creates
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 1h (sweep identification), 15m, 5m, 1m (entry)
SETUP_DEFINITION:
- Identify a liquidity sweep per Liq_Sweep_Reversal's LIQUIDITY_POOLS rules.
- The reversal displacement following the sweep must leave a 3-candle fair value gap (per FVG_Rebalance's definition) in the new direction. If no such gap forms, use Liq_Sweep_Reversal instead, which also allows an order block as an alternative entry point.
- The entry reference point is the midpoint (50%) of that fair value gap, not its far edge.
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- midpoint_close: entry-timeframe candle closes beyond the fair value gap's midpoint in the trade direction — the primary trigger, replacing an unconfirmed resting limit order at the midpoint with a confirmed close, since the parser requires a triggering candle rather than a bare resting order
- wick_reject: price wicks to or beyond the gap's midpoint and closes back in the trade direction on the same candle — a faster alternative to midpoint_close
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop placed beyond the wick extreme of the sweep candle — the primary stop
- fixed_atr: stop placed 1.3x ATR(14) beyond the sweep candle's wick extreme — use when the sweep wick is small
(all modes: clamp to broker/data minimum stop distance if too tight)
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- partial_1_5R: partial close at 1.5R, remainder to breakeven and trailed toward the next liquidity pool — the primary choice, since entering at the gap midpoint gives a tighter stop that supports a larger trailed target for the same distance
- fixed_2R: full close at a fixed 2R — the conservative alternative
FILTERS:
- Skip entry if the fair value gap has not yet formed — do not anticipate it before the displacement candle closes
- Skip entry if the gap's total range is smaller than 0.3x ATR(14) for the symbol (too small relative to typical volatility to be a meaningful imbalance)
NOTES:
- Same base mechanism as Liq_Sweep_Reversal, with the entry point narrowed specifically to the fair value gap midpoint for a tighter stop relative to the same target.
