NAME: Consolidation_Box
DESCRIPTION: False-breakout fade of a tight consolidation range ("box") — an aggressive breakout sweeps one side of the range, reverses back inside, and is faded on the retest of the box boundary
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 1h, 4h
ZONES:
- A "box" is a lookback window (default 10 candles) whose high-low range is tight relative to volatility — defined here as range <= 3x ATR(14) at the breakout bar (no numeric threshold given in source material; this is an implementation judgment call).
- A breakout candle wicks beyond the box's high or low but closes back inside the box (false breakout).
- The tradeable zone sits at the swept boundary, sized to the outer quarter of the box's range, for a retest entry.
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- wick_reject: the breakout candle itself closes back inside the box by at least 50% of its own range
- close_beyond: a subsequent candle closes beyond the zone's near edge in the trade direction
- engulfing: the entry candle's body engulfs the prior candle's body in the trade direction
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop beyond the breakout candle's wick extreme (outside the box)
- fixed_atr: stop 1.5x ATR(14) beyond the breakout candle's wick extreme
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- fixed_2R: full close at 2R
- partial_1_5R: partial at 1.5R, remainder trailed toward the opposite side of the box
- trail_only: trail behind new swing points once 1R is reached
FILTERS:
- Only trade boxes that are genuinely tight (range <= 3x ATR at the breakout bar) — wide/trending ranges are not consolidation and should not be faded.
NOTES:
- Source: algostorm.com/ict-smc-entry-models/, "Model 5: The Consolidation Box Setup". The source page frames this explicitly as a classical false-breakout/fade pattern with an ICT relabeling, and flags that distinguishing false breakouts from genuine ones "is one of trading's hardest problems" — no reliable way to know in advance which a given breakout will be. Treat this as a higher-risk counter-trend model, consistent with the source's own framing.
- Runs on the ICT registry engine (zones_consolidation_box in ict_strategies.py) — see interpreted_rules.zone_source in backtest results to confirm.
