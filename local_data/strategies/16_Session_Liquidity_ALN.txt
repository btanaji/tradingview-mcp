NAME: Session_Liquidity_ALN
DESCRIPTION: Trades the New York session's reaction to whether London swept or continued the Asian session's range
SYMBOL_DEFAULT: XAUUSD
TIMEFRAMES: 1h, 15m (session range tracking), 5m, 1m (entry)
SESSION_LOGIC:
- Asian range: the high and low of price action between 20:00-00:00 EST.
- Evaluate London (02:00-05:00 EST) relative to the Asian range: if London sweeps one side of the Asian range and continues in that same direction, flag New York (07:00-10:00 EST) as the expected reversal session. If London already reverses relative to Asia, flag New York as the expected continuation session in London's direction instead.
- Only one of these two outcomes should be flagged per day. If the flagged condition is ambiguous — for example London both sweeps and reverses within its own window without a clear net direction — skip the day.
ENTRY_CONFIRMATION: [OPTIONS]
- close_beyond: a 5m/1m candle closes beyond its own most recent minor swing point during the New York window, in the direction flagged by SESSION_LOGIC — the primary trigger for this strategy
- wick_reject: price wicks into the level that set up the New York session's reversal/continuation point and closes back in the flagged direction on the same candle — a faster alternative to close_beyond
STOP_LOSS: [OPTIONS]
- swing_based: stop placed beyond the wick extreme of the candle that set up the New York session's reversal/continuation point — the primary stop
- fixed_atr: stop placed 1.5x ATR(14) beyond that same extreme — use when that wick is unusually small
(all modes: clamp to broker/data minimum stop distance if too tight)
TAKE_PROFIT: [OPTIONS]
- fixed_2R: full close at a fixed 2R — the base case
- partial_1_5R: partial close at 1.5R, remainder to breakeven and trailed toward the opposite side of the day's range — the standard choice; see FILTERS for the forced session-end exit that applies on top of whichever mode is selected
FILTERS:
- Skip entry if the Asian range (per SESSION_LOGIC) is wider than 1.5x its 20-day average range (too wide to act as a clean liquidity pool)
- Skip entry if a scheduled high-impact news release falls inside the New York window
- Skip entry if Asia and London have already fully reversed each other before the New York window opens (the day's manipulative session already occurred)
- Force-close any still-open position at the end of the New York window regardless of which TAKE_PROFIT mode is selected (this replaces the previous "session_close_exit" TAKE_PROFIT label, which is not part of the recognized token set)
NOTES:
- Session-sequence-specific version of Liq_Sweep_Reversal / AMD_Order_Flow: Asia supplies the liquidity, London sweeps it, New York reverses or continues per SESSION_LOGIC.
